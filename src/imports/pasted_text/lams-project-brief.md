Project Title

Land Acquisition Management System (LAMS)

Organization

Revenue Department, Sub-Division Hiranagar, District Kathua, Union Territory of Jammu & Kashmir

Objective

Design a complete, enterprise-grade Government web application for managing the entire land acquisition and compensation process digitally from initiation to final payment.

The application should look like it has been developed by NIC India with the design quality of Apple Human Interface Guidelines, Government eOffice, DigiLocker, GST Portal, and PM GatiShakti, using premium glassmorphism and modern enterprise UI instead of traditional government website aesthetics.

The design should feel trustworthy, elegant, futuristic, efficient, and suitable for government officers handling confidential land acquisition records.

Design Language

Use the Jammu & Kashmir Government emblem as the design inspiration.

The website should inherit its visual identity from the emblem instead of copying it directly.
